Block Quest: Classroom Edition

Teacher guide. Weekly spelling words and Bible memory verse games for your class.

Open the game in Chrome or Edge: https://bsisco-star.github.io/block-quest-classroom/
Use the same computer and browser every time, because that's where student progress is saved.

1. First setup (about 2 minutes)

  1. The first time it opens, type your class name, your name, and a 4-digit Teacher PIN. The PIN keeps students out of Teacher Tools. Students never need a password.
  2. Type or paste your class list, one name per line or separated by commas. You can also skip this and add names later.

2. Teacher Tools

On the "Who's playing?" screen, tap the gear in the bottom-left corner and enter your PIN. If you forget the PIN, tap Forgot PIN?, hold the gear for 5 seconds, and answer the grown-up question. Then set a new PIN in Settings.

  • Students: add, rename, or remove students, change a student's avatar, or reset one student's progress.
  • Class progress: shows each student's days played this week, games finished, and whether they finished Spelling and the Bible Verse.

3. Add this week's words and verse

  1. Go to Teacher Tools, then Add this week.
  2. Type or paste the spelling words, one per line or separated by commas. You can add a practice sentence after a colon if you'd like, for example cake: We ate cake at the party.
  3. Type the memory verse exactly as students learn it, then the reference (for example, Ephesians 4:32).
  4. Tap Save this week. It becomes "this week" for everyone. Earlier weeks stay in My Weeks for practice, and nobody's progress is erased.

The game ships with sample words so you can try it. Replace them with your own words in Add this week. If someone sends you a week file, use Load week file….

4. How students play

Students tap Play, tap their name, and pick a game. The computer's voice reads words and verses aloud, so turn the volume up. Students earn gems and treasure chests: a daily chest after 3 games, streak chests, and a Big Chest for a Full Week (5 days plus Spelling plus Verse). They spend gems on avatar items and island pieces. All rewards are in the game only.

Important: back up every week. Progress is saved only in this browser on this computer, and nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's history or data, or using a different computer, will start fresh. In Teacher Tools, go to Backup and tap Download backup file. Tap Restore from a backup file to bring it back on this computer or another one.
